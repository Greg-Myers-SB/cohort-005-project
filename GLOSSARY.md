# Cadence

A platform where learners buy video courses from instructors.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A member of the staff who run the platform.
_Avoid_: using it for a Team manager

**Team manager**:
The person who bought a Team licence and hands its Seats out.
_Avoid_: team admin

### Purchases and money back

**Purchase**:
One payment for one or more Seats on a course.

**Seat**:
The right for one person to take a course. It is the smallest unit that can be bought or cancelled, and it cannot be divided.
_Avoid_: licence, coupon (a coupon is how a team Seat is handed to someone)

**Team licence**:
A Purchase of Seats that the buyer hands out to other people rather than taking themselves.

**Purchasing-power price**:
A lower price for a Seat, set by the buyer's country.

**Cancellation**:
A request to undo one Seat, raised within the Cancellation window and approved or declined by an Admin. A Seat can have only one Cancellation, the Admin's decision is final, and a learner can have only one approved Cancellation per course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, refund request, chargeback, revocation

**Cancellation window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, including the fourteenth day, in which a Cancellation may be raised.
_Avoid_: refund window

**Refund**:
The price paid for a Seat, given back in full when its Cancellation is approved. There are no partial Refunds of a Seat.
_Avoid_: Reversal

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute the cardholder raises through their bank, outside the platform. It is not something a learner raises with us.
_Avoid_: using it for any Cancellation or Refund
